PointCloud Lab

Purpose

PointCloud Lab is an educational 3D sensing prototype for exploring XYZ point clouds, synthetic surface variation, anomaly clusters, and imported inspection data.

Controls

Choose a surface preset, change point count, sensor noise, anomaly strength, point size, and color mode. You can export a sample CSV or import simple XYZ/CSV text files with columns such as x, y, z, intensity.

Limitations

Procedural points and imported files are displayed for learning only. The app is not a validated metrology, LiDAR, CT, or industrial inspection system.
